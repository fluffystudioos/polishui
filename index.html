Welcome to PolishUI

UI style reference tool for Figma, Photoshop, and Roblox UI designers. Here's how to use it:

1
Pick a category (Anime, Cartoon, Premium, RPG, Cyber, Gaming, Minimal)
2
Click a style like "Metal" or "Sakura" to preview
3
Adjust Hue/Tint sliders to change colors
4
Copy settings to Figma/CSS/Lua from the bottom panel
5
⭐ Favorite styles and find them in "Favorites" category
